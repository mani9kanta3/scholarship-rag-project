# Vivek Medical Students Scholarship Program

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/vivek-medical-students-scholarship-program
Provider: Karuna Trust
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The programme aims to empower deserving students who are meritorious and economically backward medical students who want to pursue their MBBS.

## Eligibility
A 1st year MBBS student of Bangalore Medical College or Mysore Medical College with 85% or above in 2nd PUC and an entrance exam rank of 2000 or below.
A 2nd, 3rd or 4th year MBBS student who has not received any scholarship other than VMS in previous years and has secured over 70% in the last university exam.
Annual family income not exceeding INR 3,00,000.

## Benefits
Selected applicants will receive the scholarship amount determined by the Karuna Trust.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card.
Academic transcripts.
Proof of admission.
Family income proof.
Passport size picture.

## How to Apply
Register on the platform, complete the interlinked application form, attach the necessary documents and send them to karunatrustblr@gmail.com.

## Deadline
20 November 2025.
