# AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/pragati-scholarship
Provider: All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The scheme offers 5,000 scholarships every year for degree courses and 5,000 scholarships every year for diploma courses. Students receive financial support for technical education at AICTE-approved institutions.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be girl students admitted to the 1st year, or the 2nd year through lateral entry only, of a technical diploma or degree programme.
Two females per family are eligible to apply.
The annual family income of the applicant must not exceed INR 8,00,000 during the preceding financial year.
Admission through the centralized admission process of the State or Central Government is required.

## Benefits
Selected students receive INR 50,000 per annum for college fees, books, equipment, laptops, software and desktops.
Support extends up to 3 years for first year admits and 2 years for lateral entry students.

## Documents Required
Class 10 and 12 marksheets, Aadhaar card, passport size photo, signature, income certificate, admission letter, fee receipt, bank passbook, institute certificate, caste certificate if applicable and parental declaration.

## How to Apply
Register on the NSP Portal, receive an application ID and password via mobile, log in, change the password, complete the scholarship form, upload the required documents and submit the application.

## Deadline
October 31, 2026.
