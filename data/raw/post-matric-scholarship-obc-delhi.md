# Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students, Delhi

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/post-matric-scholarship-for-obc-students-delhi
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, managed by the Department of Welfare of SC/ST/OBC
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A centrally sponsored scholarship offering financial assistance to post-matriculation and post-secondary level students. It provides monthly stipends plus fee coverage.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian citizen.
Applicants must be domiciled in Delhi.
Applicants must belong to the OBC category.
Applicants must be studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary level in a government-recognised institution.
Applicants must have an annual parent or guardian income of not more than INR 1,00,000.
Applicants must have secured an attendance of at least 75% in the previous year.

## Benefits
Day scholars receive INR 260 to INR 750 per month.
Hostelers receive INR 160 to INR 350 per month.
Full Tuition Fee, Registration Fee and Examination Fee as applicable.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card, Aadhaar-linked bank account details, certificates and marksheets from previous exams, OBC caste certificate, current fee receipts, fee structure from the institution, income certificate from the SDM or Deputy Commissioner, and a certificate confirming no other scholarship is received.

## How to Apply
Register via the e-district portal, select Aadhaar for verification, log in, choose the scholarship scheme, fill in the details, upload documents in the prescribed format and submit.

## Deadline
31 May 2026.
