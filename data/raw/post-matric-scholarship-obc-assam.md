# NSP Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students, Assam

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/post-matric-scholarship-for-obc-students-assam
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, and Directorate of Welfare of Plain Tribes & Backward Classes (DWPTBC), Government of Assam
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students, Assam is an opportunity to provide financial support to students in Class 11 and above.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must be domiciled in Assam.
Applicants must belong to the Other Backward Class (OBC) community.
Applicants must be studying in classes 11 or above in a recognised institution.
Applicants must have passed matriculation or higher secondary or any higher level examination.
Applicants must have an annual parent or guardian income of not more than INR 2,50,000 from all sources.

## Benefits
Scholarships range from INR 5,000 to INR 20,000 annually depending on the course category.
A Freeship Card is issued allowing admission to the institutions without pre-payment of tuition fees and hostel fees.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph, academic transcripts, valid caste certificate, income certificate, prior year scholarship receipt if applicable and disability certificate if applicable.

## How to Apply
Apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) via the Apply Now button. Complete the one-time registration (OTR) with Aadhaar or EID verification and face authentication.

## Deadline
30 September 2026.
