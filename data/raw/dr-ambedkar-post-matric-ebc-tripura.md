# Dr Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship for Economically Backward Classes (EBC), Tripura

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/dr-ambedkar-post-matric-scholarship-for-economically-backward-classes-ebc-tripura
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative providing financial support to EBC students in post-matriculation studies. The selected students will receive a monthly maintenance allowance of up to INR 750 and other benefits for the complete duration of the course.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must be a domicile of Tripura.
Applicants must belong to the EBC category.
Applicants must be enrolled in a post-matriculation course at a government-recognised institution.
The annual family income must be not more than INR 2,50,000.
Students pursuing the same education level in a different subject are ineligible.
Students with completed professional degrees seeking another are ineligible.
Class 11 and 12 students are ineligible.
Certain postgraduate medicine students are ineligible.

## Benefits
The monthly maintenance allowance ranges from INR 260 to INR 750 for hostellers, or INR 160 to INR 350 for day scholars, by course group.
Reader allowance for blind scholars is INR 175 for Groups A and B, INR 130 for Group C and INR 90 for Group D.
Fee reimbursement for compulsory charges.
Up to INR 900 annually for study tours.
Up to INR 1,000 for thesis charges.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, domicile certificate, passport size photograph, certificates for passed exams and previous year scholarship receipt if applicable.

## How to Apply
Apply online through the National Scholarship Portal via registration and the OTR process with face authentication.

## Deadline
15 January 2026.
