# National Overseas Scholarship Scheme for ST Students

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/national-overseas-scholarship-scheme-for-st-students
Provider: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A scholarship for ST students to pursue higher education at foreign institutes or universities. Selected candidates receive an annual maintenance allowance and various travel and study related benefits.

## Eligibility
Applicants must belong to the ST category.
For a Master's degree, the applicant must be not more than 32 years of age as of 1st July 2026, and must have scored 55% marks or equivalent in the bachelor's degree.
For a PhD, the applicant must be not more than 35 years of age as of 1st July 2026, and must have scored 55% marks or equivalent in the master's degree.
For postdoctoral research, the applicant must be not more than 38 years of age as of 1st July 2026, and must have scored 55% marks in the master's degree with an awarded PhD.
Applicants must have a family income not exceeding INR 6,00,000 per annum.

## Benefits
Annual maintenance allowance of USD 15,400 in the US or GBP 9,900 in the UK.
Annual Contingency and Equipment Allowance of USD 1,532 in the US or GBP 1,116 in the UK.
Visa fees, poll tax, medical insurance, air passage and local travel expenses.
Incidental journey expenses capped at USD 20.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph, ST certificate, PVTG certificate if applicable, admission offer, income certificate, Class 10 certificate, graduation and postgraduation marksheet, and PhD completion certificate where applicable.

## How to Apply
Register on the portal via Gmail, Mobile or Email, complete DigiLocker registration using Aadhaar, fill in the application form, upload the required documents and submit the application online.

## Deadline
30th June 2026. The status shown on the page is closed.
