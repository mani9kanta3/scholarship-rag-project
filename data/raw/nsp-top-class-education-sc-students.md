# NSP Central Sector Scholarship of Top Class Education for SC Students

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/nsp-top-class-education-scheme-for-sc-students
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative supporting SC students pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate studies in any discipline. It recognises quality education beyond Class 12 among Scheduled Caste students and provides financial assistance for tuition and living expenses.

## Eligibility
Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
Applicants must have passed the Class 12 examination with a minimum of 60% marks.
Applicants must have secured admission to a recognised college or university for a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate degree.
Applicants must have an annual income of less than INR 8,00,000 from all sources.

## Benefits
Full tuition fee and non-refundable charges. A ceiling of INR 2,00,000 per annum per student applies to private sector institutions.
Academic allowance for the first year: INR 86,000.
Academic allowance for subsequent years: INR 41,000 per year.
Payments are made by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Documents Required
Caste certificate.
Income certificate.
Bank account details.
Proof of admission.
Entrance exam scorecard if applicable.
Class 12 marksheets.
Program fee structure.

## How to Apply
Register on the portal, complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) with eKYC verification, then apply for the scholarship through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

## Deadline
31st October 2026.
