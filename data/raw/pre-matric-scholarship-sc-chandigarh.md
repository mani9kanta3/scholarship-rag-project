# NSP Pre-Matric Scholarship Scheme for SC Students (Class 9 and 10), Chandigarh

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/nsp-prematric-scholarship-scheme-for-sc-students-class-nine-and-ten-chandigarh
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India. It provides financial assistance to SC students in Classes 9 and 10. The scholarship aims to support education and reduce dropout rates during the transition from elementary to secondary school.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a domicile of Chandigarh.
Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
Applicants must be studying in Class 9 or 10 in a government school, or a school recognised by the Government, or a Central or State Board of Secondary Education.
Applicants must have an annual parental or guardian income of not more than INR 2,50,000 from all sources.
Students should not be receiving benefits under any other educational scholarship for Class 9 and 10.

## Benefits
Day scholars receive INR 225 monthly for 10 months plus an INR 750 annual books grant.
Hostellers receive INR 525 monthly for 10 months plus an INR 1,000 annual books grant.
Additional allowances are available for disabled students in private schools, from INR 160 to INR 240 monthly depending on the disability type.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card, domicile certificate, passport size photograph with signature, academic certificates, caste certificate from a Revenue Officer of Tehsildar rank minimum, family income certificate and previous year scholarship receipt for renewal.

## How to Apply
Eligible applicants can apply through the National Scholarship Portal via online registration, eKYC verification, One-Time Registration completion, then scholarship application submission.

## Deadline
15th September 2026.
