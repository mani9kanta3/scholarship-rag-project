# Karnataka Students Welfare Society Scholarship Program

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/karnataka-students-welfare-society-scholarship-program
Provider: Karnataka Students Welfare Trust
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A programme aimed at providing financial support to students in South India for higher education after high school.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian citizen.
Applicants must be a resident of Karnataka, Kerala or Tamil Nadu.
Applicants must have passed the 12th board or Pre-University Course (PUC) examination with a minimum of 60% marks.
Applicants must be enrolled or accepted for enrollment as a full-time student in an accredited institution of higher education.

## Benefits
Scholarships from INR 10,000 to INR 1,00,000, with funds sent directly to the educational institution to cover tuition and related costs.

## Documents Required
Transcripts of the last qualifying exams.
Residency proof.
Enrollment proof such as a fee receipt or admission letter.
