# Chief Minister's Overseas Scholarship Scheme for Minorities, Telangana

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/chief-ministers-overseas-scholarship-scheme-for-minorities-telangana
Provider: Minority Development Department, Government of Telangana
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative supporting students from minority communities pursuing postgraduate and doctoral degrees abroad. It offers financial assistance to 500 selected students with scholarships up to INR 20,00,000.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a domicile of Telangana.
Applicants must belong to a minority community as notified by the Government.
Applicants must be below 30 years of age as of July 1st of the application year.
Applicants must be pursuing a PG or Ph.D. in the USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, France, South Korea or Singapore.
Applicants must have secured at least 60% marks in the previous qualifying examination.
Applicants must have a valid English Proficiency Test score.
Applicants must have a total annual family income from all sources of less than INR 5,00,000.
Only one child per family is eligible.

## Benefits
A scholarship up to INR 20,00,000 or as per the admission letter, whichever is less.
A one-way economy ticket.
Eligible for an additional education loan of INR 5,00,000 from a nationalised bank.
Can supplement with research or teaching assistantship income.

## Documents Required
Caste certificate, income certificate, date of birth certificate, Aadhaar card, E-pass ID, domicile certificate, passport copy, marksheets for SSC, Intermediate, Graduation and PG, GRE or GMAT scores, TOEFL or IELTS scores, foreign university admission letter, tax assessment copy, bank passbook copy and photograph.

## How to Apply
Register on the portal, go to the Overseas Scholarship Services tab, click Registration under the Chief Minister's scheme section, complete the form with the required documents and submit.

## Deadline
19 January 2026.
