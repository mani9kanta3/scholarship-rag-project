# Post-Graduate Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/post-graduate-indira-gandhi-scholarship-for-single-girl-child
Provider: University Grants Commission (UGC), Ministry of Human Resource Development, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative for girl students up to 30 years of age who happen to be the only girl child in their family. It aims to promote education and compensate for direct educational costs.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a girl student up to 30 years of age at the time of admission.
Applicants must be the only child of the family.
Applicants must have taken admission to a regular full-time first-year master's degree course in any designated university or a post-graduate college.
Distance education mode is not covered.

## Benefits
A total of 3,000 scholarships will be awarded.
INR 36,200 per annum.
The award runs for a duration of 2 years or the full duration of the postgraduate course.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card or enrollment scanned copy.
Student photograph.
Self-attested previous academic mark sheets.
Current course year fee receipt.
Affidavit regarding single girl child status.

## How to Apply
Register on the NSP portal and complete steps 1 to 10 including registration, OTP verification and application form submission, to receive an application ID and password.

## Deadline
Closed. The scholarship closing date was 31st December 2022.
