# National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMS)

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/nmms-scholarship
Provider: Ministry of Education, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative providing financial support to meritorious students from economically weaker sections to help them complete school education.

## Eligibility
Students from any community studying in Government, Government-aided, or local body schools are eligible.
The parental income must not exceed INR 3,50,000 per annum.
Applicants must have scored at least 55% marks, or 50% for SC and ST, in their Class VII exams to qualify for the selection test.

## Benefits
The selected students can get INR 1,000 per month for a year.

## Documents Required
Fee payment challan, SEB copy only.
Aadhaar card.
Passport size photograph.
Income certificate obtained from a competent authority.
Class 7 report card or equivalent proof.
Caste certificate.
Disability certificate if applicable.

## How to Apply
You can apply for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) online, either as a fresh applicant or for renewal, on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). Registration on NSP is mandatory before applying for the examination. State specific official websites are listed for submitting applications.

## Deadline
Student application open till 31 August 2025.
