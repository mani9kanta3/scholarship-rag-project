# Swami Dayanand India Scholarship

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/swami-dayanand-india-scholarship
Provider: The Swami Dayanand Education Foundation
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
This scholarship provides financial assistance to meritorious students pursuing undergraduate professional courses in India.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be enrolled in the first year of a full-time undergraduate professional degree programme in India.
Applicants must have secured a minimum of 80% marks or equivalent CGPA in Class 12.
Applicants must have an annual family income of INR 12 lakh or less from all sources.
Applicants must be pursuing one of the eligible professional courses listed: Engineering and Technology B.Tech. or B.E., Medicine MBBS, Dentistry BDS, Nursing B.Sc. Nursing or B.Nursing, Pharmacy B.Pharm. or Pharm.D, Architecture B.Arch., Veterinary Science BVSc and AH.
Students who have taken a gap year after Class 12 and students already pursuing a second year or higher are not eligible.

## Benefits
Selected candidates will receive a scholarship of up to INR 50,000 per academic year.

## Documents Required
Latest photograph, admission letter or college ID card, Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates, JEE or NEET result letter, fee receipts, family income proof, electricity or water bill, agricultural land documents if applicable and the SDEF Scholar Pledge Form.
