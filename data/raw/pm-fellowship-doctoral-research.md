# Prime Minister's Fellowship Scheme for Doctoral Research

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/prime-minister-s-fellowship-scheme-for-doctoral-research
Provider: Science & Engineering Research Board (SERB), Department of Science and Technology, Government of India and Confederation of Indian Industry (CII)
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The fellowship supports full-time Ph.D. scholars with an amount of up to INR 10,41,000 annually for each candidate who is researching in the areas of Science, Technology, Engineering, Agriculture or Medicine. It is a public-private partnership offering support for a maximum of four years.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must have taken admission in a full-time Ph.D. degree in the last 14 months from the date of submission of applications in any recognized Indian University, Institute or Research Laboratory.
The research must have practical relevance and industrial application.
Applicants must have completed all required academic qualifications.
Applicants must have an industry partner to sponsor 50% of the fellowship.

## Benefits
A total of 100 fellowships of INR 10,41,000 per annum will be awarded to students.
SERB provides INR 5,20,000 per annum including a 10% management fee, and the industry co-applicant matches the remaining 50%.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph, no objection certificate, Ph.D. enrolment or registration certificate, high school and intermediate certificates, graduation and postgraduation certificates, diploma copies, host institute profile, supervisor biodata, partner company profile, industry mentor biodata, undertakings from the supervisor and the head of the organization, and an industry partner letter detailing the research problem.

## How to Apply
Click Apply Now, register via Gmail, mobile or email, go to student registration, download the proposal and checklist, secure a company partnership, collect documents as digital copies, complete the online application form and submit.

## Deadline
Always Open. Last date for online submission of form is within 14 months from registration in the Ph.D.
