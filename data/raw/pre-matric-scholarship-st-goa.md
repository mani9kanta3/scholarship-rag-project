# NSP Centrally Sponsored Scheme of Pre-Matric Scholarship for ST Students (Class 9 and 10), Goa

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/nsp-centrally-sponsored-scheme-of-pre-matric-scholarship-for-st-students-class-9-and-10-goa
Provider: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity provided by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India, to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Tribe (ST) students in Goa who are enrolled in Classes 9 or 10.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must be a domicile of Goa.
Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Tribe (ST) community.
Applicants must be a regular full-time student in Class 9 or 10 in a government or recognised school.
Applicants must have an annual family income of less than INR 2,50,000.
Applicants must not be receiving any other centrally funded Pre-Matric Scholarship.

## Benefits
Day Scholars: INR 2,250 annually plus an INR 750 books or ad hoc grant.
Hostelers: INR 5,250 annually plus an INR 1,000 books or ad hoc grant.
Additional Divyangjan allowance: INR 800 per month for hostelers or INR 600 per month for day scholars.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph, Aadhaar number, domicile certificate, caste certificate, family income certificate and disability certificate if applicable.

## How to Apply
Eligible applicants can apply for the scholarship through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) following a ten step registration and application process including OTR registration and document upload.

## Deadline
31 October 2025.
