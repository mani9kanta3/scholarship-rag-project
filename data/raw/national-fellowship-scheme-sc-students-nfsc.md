# National Fellowship Scheme for Scheduled Caste Students (NFSC)

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/national-fellowship-scheme-for-scheduled-caste-students
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The scheme provides financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students pursuing M.Phil or Ph.D. degrees in Science, Humanities, or Social Science at UGC-recognised institutions. It offers 2,000 new fellowships per year: 1,500 for Junior Research Fellows in Humanities/Social Sciences and 500 for Junior Research Fellows in Science.

## Eligibility
Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Caste category as per state government lists.
Applicants must be pursuing regular full-time M.Phil/Ph.D. courses at UGC-recognised universities.
Applicants must have qualified in either the National Eligibility Test (NET) or the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) examination.

## Benefits
INR 37,000 per month for the initial two years as Junior Research Fellow.
INR 42,000 per month for the remaining tenure as Senior Research Fellow.
Contingency allowances of INR 10,000 to INR 20,500 annually for Humanities and Social Sciences, and INR 12,000 to INR 25,000 for Sciences.
House Rent Allowance as per rules.
Reader assistance of INR 2,000 per month for disabled candidates.

## Documents Required
Caste certificate.
Disability certificate if applicable.
Marksheets.
Aadhaar card.
Passport size photograph.
Bank details.
PAN card.
Admission letter.
Research proposal.
NET/UGC-CSIR NET scorecard.

## How to Apply
Click Apply Now and register using Gmail, mobile number or email. Selection is made by the responsible government authorities based on the merit of each UGC-NET-JRF or UGC-CSIR NET-JRF examination.

## Deadline
20 December 2026.
