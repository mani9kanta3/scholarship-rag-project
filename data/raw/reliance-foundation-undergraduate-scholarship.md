# Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarships

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/reliance-foundation-undergraduate-scholarships
Provider: Reliance Foundation
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The program aims to support first-year undergraduate students across all streams. Since 2022-2023 it has annually supported 5,100 students, including 5,000 meritorious undergraduate scholars from underprivileged backgrounds. The scholarship provides financial aid throughout the degree program, alongside alumni network access and career support.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian citizen.
Applicant must have passed class 12 with minimum 60% marks.
Applicants must be currently enrolled in the 1st year of a regular full-time degree course in any stream.
Applicant's family income must be less than INR 15 Lakhs. Preference will be given to applicants having family income of less than INR 2.5 Lakhs.
An aptitude test is compulsory.
Students in 2nd year or higher are not eligible.
Non-regular modes such as online or hybrid are not eligible.
A diploma after class 10 is not eligible.
Two year or six year programs are not eligible.

## Benefits
Students will receive a scholarship amount of up to INR 2,00,000 over the duration of the UG degree programme.
Access to the alumni network and career support is included.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph, proof of permanent address, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, bonafide student certificate, disability certificate if applicable, family income proof, PAN and bank details.

## How to Apply
Visit the portal, register or log in, click Start Application, complete the form, upload the documents, accept the terms, preview and submit. An aptitude test is required after submission.

## Deadline
October 7, 2025.
