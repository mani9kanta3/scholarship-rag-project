# Mahatma Jyothiba Phule Overseas Vidya Nidhi Scholarship, Telangana

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/mahatma-jyothiba-phule-overseas-vidya-nidhi-telangana
Provider: Backward Classes Welfare Department, Government of Telangana
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative for BC and EBC category students pursuing UG, PG and Ph.D. level courses abroad. Winners receive scholarship funds, a one-way economy flight and visa coverage.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a Telangana resident.
Applicants must belong to the Backward Class (BC) or Economically Backward Class (EBC) category.
Applicants must not be more than 35 years of age as on 1st July of the year of advertisement.
Applicants must have secured a minimum of 60% in the previous qualifying examination.
Applicants must be studying in the USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Singapore, Germany, New Zealand, Japan, France or South Korea.
Applicants must have an annual family income below INR 5,00,000 from all sources.
Applicants must have the required English proficiency test scores: TOEFL 60, IELTS 6.0, GRE 260, GMAT 500 or PTE 50.

## Benefits
A scholarship amount of up to INR 20,00,000 or as mentioned in the admission letter, whichever is less.
A one-way flight ticket in economy class and visa charges.

## Documents Required
TOEFL, IELTS, GRE, GMAT or PTE scorecard.
Degrees and certificates.

## How to Apply
Click Apply Now, register via Gmail, Mobile or Email, go to Registration, provide the details and documents and submit the application.

## Deadline
15 October 2025.
