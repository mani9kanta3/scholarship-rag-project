# Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC, EBC and DNT Students, Sikkim

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/post-matric-scholarship-for-obc-ebc-and-dnt-students-sikkim-2023-24
Provider: Department of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students belonging to the BC, EWS, and DNT communities of the state and studying at the post-matric level of education.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be Indian nationals domiciled in Sikkim.
Applicants must belong to the BC, EWS or DNT communities.
Applicants must be studying post-matriculation in recognized institutions.
Applicants must have an annual parent or guardian income of not more than INR 2,50,000 from all sources.
Applicants must have passed matriculation or higher examinations from recognized boards.

## Benefits
Awards vary by course category.
Group 1 professional courses provide INR 10,000 academic allowance and INR 10,000 tuition fees annually, INR 20,000 in total.
Group 2 offers INR 8,000 and INR 5,000 respectively.
Group 3 provides INR 6,000 and INR 2,000.
Group 4 offers INR 5,000.
Freeship cards enable fee-free attendance initially.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph.
Attested academic transcripts.
Valid caste certificate duly signed by an authorised Revenue Officer.
Income certificate from the state authority.
Previous year's scholarship receipt if applicable.

## How to Apply
Register via the Apply Now link, complete the registration steps, go to the Applicant Corner New Registration tab, complete Aadhaar verification with an OTP, then submit the Fresh Application with the required details and documents.

## Deadline
15th March 2024. The status shown on the page is Closed.
