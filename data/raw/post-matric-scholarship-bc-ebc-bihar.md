# Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) for BC/EBC Category, Bihar

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/post-matric-scholarship-pms-for-bc-ebc-category-bihar
Provider: Education Department, Government of Bihar
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity for BC and EBC category students in Bihar pursuing post-matric education. The selected candidates will receive a scholarship of up to INR 15,000 annually, plus maintenance allowance, fee reimbursement, study tour charges, thesis typing, book allowance and book bank facilities.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a Bihar domicile.
Applicants must belong to the BC or EBC category.
Applicants must have an annual family income of not more than INR 1,00,000 from all sources.

## Benefits
Awards vary by course level.
Intermediate: INR 2,000.
Graduation: INR 5,000.
Post Graduation, M.Phil. or Ph.D.: INR 5,000.
ITI: INR 5,000.
Polytechnic or Diploma: INR 10,000.
Professional or Technical: INR 15,000.

## Documents Required
Passport photo in jpeg under 50KB, Class 10 and 12 certificates, graduation and postgraduate certificates if applicable, residential certificate, caste certificate, income certificate, bonafide certificate, institution fee receipt and last exam passing certificate. All documents must be uploaded in PDF format and under 150 to 400 KB.

## How to Apply
Click Apply, register or log in, enter the OTR number, fill in the required details, upload the documents and submit via the NSP portal.

## Deadline
15th September 2026.
