# NSP Merit Cum Means Scholarship for Professional and Technical Courses (Minorities)

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/nsp-merit-cum-means-scholarship-for-professional-and-technical-courses-cs-minorities
Provider: Ministry of Minority Affairs (MoMA), Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative for minority community students who are pursuing technical or professional courses at the undergraduate or postgraduate level. It offers financial assistance to meritorious and underprivileged students belonging to minority communities.

## Eligibility
Applicants must belong to Minority communities (Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Jain, and Parsis/Zoroastrians).
Applicants must be pursuing a technical or professional course at the undergraduate or postgraduate level from a recognized institution.
Applicants must have secured at least 50% marks or equivalent grade in the previous final examination.
Applicants must have an annual family income of less than INR 2.50 lakhs from all sources.

## Benefits
INR 20,000 per annum subject to actual whichever is less, for both Hostellers and Day Scholars.
INR 1,000 per month for ten months for a Hosteller.
INR 500 per month for ten months for a Day Scholar.
Full course fee is reimbursed for 85 listed institutes.

## Documents Required
Domicile Certificate.
Student Photograph.
Self Declaration of minority community certificate.
Self-attested previous academic marksheet.
Fee receipt.
Aadhaar Enrollment or Card copy.
Income certificate.
Proof of bank account.

## How to Apply
A ten step process. Click Apply Now, go to the Applicant Corner, select New Registration, and complete registration on the National Scholarship Portal with personal details, bank information and Aadhaar verification.

## Deadline
Closed.
