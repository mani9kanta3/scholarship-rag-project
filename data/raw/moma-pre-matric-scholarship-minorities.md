# MOMA Pre-Matric Scholarship for Minorities

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/moma-scholarships
Provider: Ministry of Minority Affairs (MOMA), Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
One of four scholarship schemes for minority students run by the Ministry of Minority Affairs. The Pre-Matric scheme supports Class 9 and 10 students. Support covers tuition, admission fees and maintenance allowances.

## Eligibility
Applicants must belong to a recognized minority: Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain or Parsi.
Applicants must be studying in Class 9 or 10.
A minimum of 50% marks is required.
The family income must be INR 1,00,000 or less.

## Benefits
INR 500 admission fee annually.
INR 350 per month tuition.
Maintenance of INR 600 per month for hostellers or INR 100 per month for day scholars.

## Documents Required
Passport photo, income certificate, community certificate which is self-certified if the applicant is 18 or above, previous marksheet, bank details, Aadhaar card and residential certificate. Students need to upload documents online if the scholarship amount exceeds INR 50,000.

## How to Apply
Visit the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in, register as a new user via the OTR process with mobile verification, complete the One Time Registration including face authentication via the mobile app, then apply for the specific scholarship with the required documents.
