# State Merit Scholarship for UG Girls Students, Haryana

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/state-merit-scholarship-for-ug-girls-students-haryana
Provider: Directorate of Higher Education, Haryana
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity offered by the Directorate of Higher Education, Haryana to female students of the state who are pursuing general undergraduate degree courses.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a domicile of Haryana.
Applicants must have passed Class 12 with at least 60% marks.
Applicants must be pursuing a general degree course at a government college.
Candidates in correspondence or open universities are ineligible.

## Benefits
Selected female candidates will receive INR 3,000 per annum.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card, photograph, signature, income certificate, Haryana domicile certificate, caste certificate, Class 10 and Class 12 certificates, Parivar Pehchan Patra, fee receipt, last exam passed certificate, BPL certificate if applicable and father's death certificate if applicable.

## How to Apply
A ten step online process on the portal including registration, Parivar Pehchan Patra verification with an OTP, department and college selection, and scholarship application submission with course and eligibility details.

## Deadline
30 November 2025.
