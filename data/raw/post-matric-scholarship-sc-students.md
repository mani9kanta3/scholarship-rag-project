# Post Matric Scholarship for SC Students

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/post-matric-scholarship-for-sc-students
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India for SC students of Class 11 and above. The key objective of the scholarship is to provide/supplement financial assistance to SC students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary states to enable them to complete their education.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Caste category as determined by the state/union territory (UT).
Applicants must be studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary level i.e. Class 11 to Ph.D. level.
Applicants must have passed matriculation or higher secondary or any higher examination of a recognised University or Board.
Applicants must have an annual family income of less than INR 2.5 lakhs.

## Benefits
The selected students will receive variable awards. These include maintenance allowance, fee reimbursement, study tour charges, thesis typing charges, book allowance and book bank facility. Additional allowances are given to SC students with disabilities.

## Documents Required
Domicile certificate.
Passport size photograph.
Scheduled Caste certificate duly signed by an authorised Revenue Officer.
Income declaration and parents'/guardians' certificate.

## How to Apply
An eight step process. Click Apply Now, read the guidelines, select the state and the scholarship details, provide bank details, select the identification method, verify the mobile number with an OTP, log in and complete the form, and receive an application ID and password. Applications are made on the National Scholarship Portal at www.scholarships.gov.in.
