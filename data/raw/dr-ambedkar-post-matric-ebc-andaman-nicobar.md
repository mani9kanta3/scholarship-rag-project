# Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship for EBC Students, Andaman and Nicobar

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/dr-ambedkar-post-matric-scholarship-for-ebc-students-andaman-and-nicobar
Provider: Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A government initiative offering financial aid to economically disadvantaged students from the Union Territory pursuing higher education beyond Class 10. It provides a monthly maintenance allowance and other support.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must be domiciled in Andaman and Nicobar.
Applicants must be of the General category, that is not SC, ST or OBC.
Applicants must have a combined annual income not exceeding INR 1,00,000.
Applicants cannot receive other scholarships simultaneously.
Applicants cannot pursue duplicate degree levels in different subjects.
Professional field transitions may be considered with restrictions.

## Benefits
The maintenance allowance varies by course group.
Group 1 hostellers receive INR 750 per month.
Group 2 receive INR 510 per month.
Group 3 receive INR 400 per month.
Group 4 receive INR 260 per month.
It also covers fees, study tour charges up to INR 900 and thesis typing up to INR 1,000.
Blind scholars receive additional reader charges.

## Documents Required
Domicile certificate.
Caste certificate if applicable.
Disability certificate if required.
Attested copies of previous qualifications.
Copy of the annual family income certificate for the current financial year.
Bank passbook copy.

## How to Apply
Register via the application portal, complete the NSP one-time registration with mobile verification and OTP, then select the scholarship and upload the required documents through the Apply for Scholarship option.

## Deadline
31 October 2025.
