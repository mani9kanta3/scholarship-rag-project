# Google PhD Fellowship India Program

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/google-phd-fellowship-india-program
Provider: Google
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Google PhD Fellowship India Program is an opportunity offered by Google to support promising Ph.D. candidates of all backgrounds who seek to influence the future of technology.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be enrolled full-time in the Ph.D. program.
Applicants must have obtained an undergraduate or master's degree from an Indian university.
Applicants must be a professional who is employed with a registered organisation in India.
Google employees and their household members are not eligible.
Students with comparable industry awards and past awardees are not eligible.

## Benefits
Early-stage PhD candidates receive an up to 4 year fellowship with USD 50,000 plus a Google research mentor.
Late-stage PhD candidates receive a 1 year fellowship with USD 10,000 plus a Google research mentor.

## Documents Required
A short one page resume or CV of the student applicant's primary PhD program advisor.
Available transcripts, that is mark sheets, from the first year or semester of the Bachelor's degree to date.
Two to three letters of recommendation from people familiar with the applicant's work.

## How to Apply
A university nomination is required. Candidates must contact their department or graduate school for the university's internal nomination process.
