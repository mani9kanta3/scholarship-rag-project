# Begum Hazrat Mahal National Scholarship

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/begum-hazrat-mahal-national-scholarship
Provider: Maulana Azad Education Foundation
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A national level scholarship for girl students from minority communities, that is Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain and Parsi. The program provides financial assistance to support meritorious students through their secondary education.

## Eligibility
Applicants studying in classes 9 to 12 can apply.
Applicants must have secured at least 50% marks or equivalent grades in aggregate in the previous class.
The annual family income of the candidate must not be more than INR 2 Lakh from all sources.
The applicant must belong to one of the six notified minority communities.

## Benefits
For Classes 9 and 10: INR 10,000 annually, which will be released in two instalments of INR 5,000 for each student quarterly.
For Classes 11 and 12: INR 12,000 annually, which will be released in two instalments of INR 6,000 for each student quarterly.
It covers books, stationery, tuition fees and lodging or boarding charges.

## Documents Required
Passport photo, school verification form, previous mark sheet, income certificate, minority community self-declaration, bank passbook copy and Aadhaar card or alternative ID.

## How to Apply
Register on the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in, complete the student registration form, log in, fill in the scholarship application with bank and income details, upload the documents and submit.
