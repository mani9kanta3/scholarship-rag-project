# SOF Girl Child Scholarship Scheme (GCSS)

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/sof-girl-child-scholarship-scheme-gcss
Provider: Science Olympiad Foundation (SOF)
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity provided by the Science Olympiad Foundation (SOF) for female students studying in Classes 1 to 10. The scholarship aims to provide financial assistance to students from economically disadvantaged families so they can continue their school education.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a female currently studying in classes 1 to 10 in a recognised school or institution.
Applicants must have secured at least 60% marks or equivalent in the previous academic year.
Applicants must have a monthly family income of less than INR 25,000.

## Benefits
300 selected girl children across India will receive INR 5,000 each annually.

## Documents Required
A signed copy of the previous year's report card from the recommending school's principal.

## How to Apply
The schools will send a proposal recommending a female student for the scholarship. The school will send the duly signed recommendation by the Head Master, Headmistress or Principal to SOF at the address provided on the page.

## Deadline
31 December 2025.
