# Pre-Matric Scholarship for SC Students (Class 9 and 10), Delhi

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/prematric-scholarship-for-sc-students-class-nine-and-ten-delhi
Provider: Department for the Welfare of SC/ST/OBC/Minority, Government of NCT of Delhi
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Caste (SC) students who are studying in Class 9 and 10. The key objective of the scholarship is to minimise the incidence of dropout among the SC category of students.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian citizen.
Applicants must be a domicile of Delhi.
Applicants must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
Applicants must be a full-time student in Class 9 or 10 from a recognised institution.
Applicants must have a family income of less than INR 2,50,000 per annum.

## Benefits
Day Scholar: INR 225 monthly plus an INR 750 annual book grant.
Hosteller: INR 525 monthly plus an INR 1,000 annual book grant.
A consolidated academic allowance and an extra allowance for disabled students.

## Documents Required
One passport size photograph with the student's signature.
Self-attested copies of certificates and mark sheets for previously passed exams.
Certificate of caste signed by an authorised Revenue Officer, not below the rank of Tehsildar.
The income certificate of the parents.
Academic transcripts.

## How to Apply
Click on the Apply Now button, fill in the required details to register, go to Apply Services, select the Aadhaar card for the document type and upload the required documents in the prescribed format.

## Deadline
31st January 2026.
