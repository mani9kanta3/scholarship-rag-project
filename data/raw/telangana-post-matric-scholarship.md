# Post-Matric Scholarship (PMS) for SC/ST/BC/Disabled Category, Telangana

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/telangana-post-matric-scholarship-pms-for-sc-st-bc-disabled-welfare
Provider: Government of Telangana
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative supporting students from SC, ST, BC and Disabled categories in higher education. The program offers tuition fee reimbursement for intermediate, ITI, polytechnic, professional, graduate, postgraduate and Ph.D. courses. Maintenance or mess charges are also provided monthly.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be Indian nationals and Telangana domiciles.
Applicants must belong to the SC, ST, BC, EBC or Disabled Welfare categories.
Applicants must have passed Class 10.
Applicants must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance at the end of each quarter.
The annual family income limit is INR 2,00,000 for the Scheduled Caste (SC/ST) category.
The annual family income limit is INR 1,50,000 for Backward Classes (BC) of rural areas.
The annual family income limit is INR 2,00,000 for Backward Classes (BC) of urban areas.

## Benefits
Reimbursement of tuition fees ranging from INR 850 for intermediate courses to INR 6,90,000 for medical courses per annum.
Variable monthly maintenance charges.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph.
Aadhaar card.
Caste certificate.
Disability certificate if applicable.
Class 10 marksheets and certificates.
First page of the bank passbook.
Study or bonafide certificate for seven years.
CET allotment order for CET courses only.

## How to Apply
Click Apply Now, register via Gmail, Mobile or Email, review the instructions and checkboxes, enter SSC details, verify credentials, complete the application form, upload documents and submit.

## Deadline
20th April 2026.
