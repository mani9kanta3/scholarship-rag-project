# Sitaram Jindal Foundation Scholarship Scheme

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/sitaram-jindal-foundation-scholarship-scheme
Provider: The Sitaram Jindal Foundation (NGO)
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity provided by the Sitaram Jindal Foundation for students from Class 11 to the postgraduate level. It aims to provide financial assistance to underprivileged students pursuing various degree and diploma courses.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be below 30 years of age.
Applicants must be a student of class 11, 12, ITI, Diploma, graduation or postgraduation, in General Fields, Medical or Engineering.
Applicants must have an income up to INR 4,00,000 for salaried employees, and up to INR 2,50,000 for others.
Final year students are ineligible.

## Benefits
Covers tuition fees and study materials.
Assists with accommodation costs for hostellers.
Rewards good grades, as the scholarship depends on academic performance.
The scholarship renews throughout the course and is disbursed biannually.

## Documents Required
Academic certificates, Class 10 examination result, income proof, proof of date of birth, certificate regarding admission and annual fee, copy of the student's bank passbook and hostel certificates as applicable.

## How to Apply
Click on the Apply Now button, register and fill in the required details, fill in name and mobile number and send the OTP, create a password and register, log in and click Apply Scholarship, then fill in all the mentioned details.

## Deadline
Always Open. Applications are received round the year.
