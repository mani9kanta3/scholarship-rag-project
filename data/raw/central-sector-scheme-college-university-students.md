# Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/central-sector-scheme-of-scholarship-for-college-and-university-students
Provider: Ministry of Education, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An opportunity to provide financial assistance to meritorious students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds for higher education. The scheme is administered through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must have passed Class 12 from a recognised institution with a minimum score of 80% in their chosen stream.
Applicants must be currently enrolled in an undergraduate (UG) or postgraduate (PG) program in a professional field at a college/institution recognised by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) or other relevant regulatory bodies.
Applicants must have an annual parental/family income not exceeding INR 4,50,000.

## Benefits
Graduation 1st, 2nd and 3rd year regular courses: INR 12,000 per annum.
Graduation 4th and 5th year professional courses: INR 20,000 per annum.
Graduation up to 3rd year technical courses: INR 12,000 per annum.
Postgraduation all courses: INR 20,000 per annum.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card.
Class 12 marksheet and certificates.
Income certificate.
Caste certificate if applicable.
Disability certificate if applicable.

## How to Apply
Eligible applicants can apply by registering on the National Scholarship Portal, logging in, completing the One-Time Registration (OTR), and submitting the scholarship application through www.scholarships.gov.in.

## Deadline
Closed.
