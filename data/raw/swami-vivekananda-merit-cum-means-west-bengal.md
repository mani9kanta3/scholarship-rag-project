# Swami Vivekananda Merit Cum Means Scholarship Scheme for Minorities, West Bengal

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/swami-vivekananda-merit-cum-means-scholarship-scheme-for-minorities-west-bengal
Provider: West Bengal Minorities Development & Finance Corporation (WBMDFC), Government of West Bengal
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Financial support for minority students pursuing education from higher secondary through postgraduation, including diploma and professional courses like Diploma (Polytechnic), Diploma in Pharmacy, B.Ed., D.El.Ed. and M.Ed.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a permanent West Bengal resident studying in a recognized institution.
Annual household income must not exceed INR 2,50,000.
Minimum marks required are 60% for Higher Secondary to the Graduation level.
Minimum marks required are 53% for the Post-graduation level and for an Honours subject at Graduation level.
Minimum marks required are 55% for the Graduation Level in the case of engineering students.

## Benefits
Selected students will receive an award of up to INR 60,000.

## Documents Required
Proof of domicile.
Income certificate.
Academic mark sheet.
Bank passbook linked to Aadhaar.
Valid Aadhaar Card.

## How to Apply
Register online, access the Student Panel, select Fresh Registration or the renewal option, choose the district, complete the application, then print and submit it to the institution with a bank passbook copy.

## Deadline
31st May 2026.
