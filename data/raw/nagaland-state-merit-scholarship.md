# Nagaland State Merit Scholarship

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/nagaland-state-merit-scholarship
Provider: Department of Higher Education, Nagaland
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
An initiative of the Department of Higher Education, Nagaland, for students in Class 11 and above. It aims to improve literacy and education levels by providing financial assistance to selected students.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a domicile of Nagaland or permanently settled in Nagaland.
Applicants must be pursuing studies at the post-matric level in a recognised institution.
For Class 11 or Diploma 1st year, a minimum of 80% in Class 10 is required.
For Bachelor's degree 1st year, a minimum of 80% in Class 12 is required.
For Engineering 2nd year through lateral entry, a minimum of 80% in the Diploma is required.
For PG 1st year, a minimum of 70% at the Graduation level is required.

## Benefits
Selected candidates will receive financial assistance to continue their higher studies.

## Documents Required
Mark sheets and admit cards as attested copies, ST or Indigenous certificate attested, admission receipt attested, school or college registration certificate attested, front page of the bank passbook and a photocopy of the Aadhaar card. For renewal, a merged mark sheets file, Part B Form if studying outside Nagaland, hosteller certificate, admission receipt, bank passbook and Aadhaar card.

## How to Apply
Click Apply Now, register via Gmail, Mobile or Email, complete registration, fill in the details, upload the documents and submit online. Students outside Nagaland must print the application form and submit it with document copies to the Directorate of Higher Education.

## Deadline
31 October 2025.
