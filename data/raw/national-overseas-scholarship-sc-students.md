# National Overseas Scholarship Scheme for SC Students

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/national-overseas-scholarship-sc-students
Provider: The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
This flagship initiative is designed to support meritorious students from historically marginalized communities, including Scheduled Castes (SC), Denotified and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNTs), Landless Agricultural Labourers, and Traditional Artisans, in pursuing Master's and Ph.D. programs at top universities abroad.

## Eligibility
Applicants must belong to the SC, DNT or SNT, LAL, or TA groups with a valid certificate.
Applicants must score a minimum of 60% in the Bachelor's degree for a Master's, or in the Master's degree for a Ph.D., or 55% for Post-Doctoral study.
The family income must not exceed INR 8 lakh annually.
Applicants must hold an unconditional admission from a QS Top 500 university.
The age limit is 35 years or less for a Master's, 40 years or less for a Ph.D., and 45 years or less for Post-Doctoral study, as of April 1.
Only two children per family can apply, however only one child is eligible per year.
Applicants must be an Indian citizen with a valid passport.
No concurrent foreign scholarships are allowed.

## Benefits
Tuition fees paid directly to the overseas university.
Annual maintenance of USD 15,400 for non-UK, or GBP 9,900 for the UK.
Contingency allowance of USD 1,500 for non-UK, or GBP 1,100 for the UK.
One economy class airfare ticket.
Visa fees, registration and research or library fees covered.

## Documents Required
Aadhaar card, Class 10 Certificate, Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, ITR proof, unconditional admission offer letter, academic mark sheets, degree certificates, photograph, scanned signature, employer's NOC if employed, passport copy, bond with two sureties, solvency certificates and medical certificate.

## How to Apply
Register at the official NOS portal at nosmsje.gov.in, complete the online application form, upload the required documents, submit and save the reference number.

## Deadline
First round runs from 15th February to 31st March. Second round runs from 1st September to 15th October, tentatively.
