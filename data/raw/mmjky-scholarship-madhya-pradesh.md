# Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan (Shiksha Protsahan) Yojana, Madhya Pradesh

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/mmjky-scholarship
Provider: Madhya Pradesh Government, Chief Minister Public Welfare (Education Promotion) Scheme
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Mukhyamantri Jan Kalyan (Shiksha Protsahan) Yojana provides free higher education support to eligible students whose parent is registered as an unorganised worker.

## Eligibility
The parent must be registered as an unorganised worker with the Labour Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh.
For Engineering, a rank up to 1,50,000 in JEE Main is required.
For Medical and Dental, admission must be through NEET in government colleges or private medical colleges in MP.
For Law, admission must be through CLAT or institutional entrance exams in NLUs or Delhi University.
Undergraduate, postgraduate, polytechnic diploma and ITI courses are covered.

## Benefits
The Madhya Pradesh Government bears the eligible education fee of selected students.
It covers admission and academic fees.
For Engineering in a private college the cover is INR 1.5 lakh or the actual payable fee, whichever is lower.
Medical service bonds are required. Government colleges require 2 years with a bond of INR 10 lakh. Private colleges require 5 years with a bond of INR 25 lakh.

## Documents Required
Parent's unorganised worker registration certificate, Class 10 and 12 marksheet, entrance examination marksheet, fee receipt, Aadhaar number, Aadhaar-linked bank account, category certificate and Samagra ID.

## How to Apply
Register on the Medhavi Kalyan Portal, fill in demographic and contact details, log in with the credentials, complete the application with institutional and course information, upload the required documents, lock and submit online, then forward it to the institute for verification.

## Deadline
August 20, 2026.
