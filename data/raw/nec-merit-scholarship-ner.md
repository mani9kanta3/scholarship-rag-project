# NSP NEC Merit Scholarship for Students of NER

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/nec-merit-scholarship-for-students-of-ner
Provider: North Eastern Council (NEC), Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Financial assistance to meritorious students from the North Eastern Region (NER) of India for pursuing higher professional courses in recognised Indian institutions.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian citizen and a permanent resident of Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim or Tripura.
Applicants must be enrolled in a diploma, degree or postgraduate course, or be registered in a M.Phil. or Ph.D. program.
Applicants must have scored a minimum of 60% marks in the qualifying examinations.
Applicants must have an annual family income of less than INR 8,00,000 from all sources.

## Benefits
Diploma: INR 20,000 per annum.
Degree: INR 22,000 per annum.
PG: INR 25,000 per annum.
M.Phil or Ph.D.: INR 30,000 per annum.

## Documents Required
Permanent residence certificate from the Deputy Commissioner.
Income certificate from the Competent Authority.
Bank account passbook in the applicant's name.
Marksheet of the previous qualifying examination.
Synopsis for M.Phil and Ph.D. candidates.

## How to Apply
Click on the Apply Now button, register via Gmail, Mobile or Email, go to the Students option, complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) on the National Scholarship Portal, then select the scholarship and upload the documents.

## Deadline
15 December 2025. The overall status shown on the page is Closed.
