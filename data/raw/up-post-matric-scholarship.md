# Post-Matric Scholarship for General, Minorities, SC, ST and OBC, Uttar Pradesh

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/postmatric-scholarship-for-general-minorities-sc-st-and-obc-uttar-pradesh
Provider: Government of Uttar Pradesh
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Post-Matric Scholarship for General, Minorities, SC, ST, and OBC, Uttar Pradesh is offered by the Government of Uttar Pradesh. It aims to provide financial support towards meritorious and needy students pursuing post-matric studies.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be a resident of Uttar Pradesh.
Applicants must belong to one of the following categories: General, Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), Minorities.
Applicants must be pursuing studies from class 11 to the postgraduate level in recognised institutions.
For the SC and ST category the annual family income must be less than INR 2,50,000 from all sources.
For the General, Minorities and OBC category the annual family income must be less than INR 2,00,000 from all sources.

## Benefits
Day Scholar and Hosteler amounts vary by category and group from 1 to 4.
Hostelers receive up to INR 13,500 per year.
Day scholars receive up to INR 7,000 per year.

## Documents Required
A duly filled application form, marksheets and certificates of the last qualifying examination, Aadhaar Card, caste certificate if applicable, income certificate, fee receipt and admission proof.

## How to Apply
Online registration via scholarship.up.gov.in, followed by OTR generation and application form submission with the required documents to the institution.

## Deadline
30 October 2025 for Classes 11 and 12. 20 December 2025 for all except classes 11 and 12.
