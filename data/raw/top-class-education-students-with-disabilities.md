# Scholarship for Top Class Education for Students with Disabilities

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/scholarships-for-top-class-education-for-students-with-disabilities
Provider: Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
Financial assistance to students with disabilities to support their higher education, for diploma, graduate and postgraduate courses from premier institutions like IITs, NITs and Institutes of Excellence.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be an Indian national.
Applicants must have a benchmark disability of 40% or more under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
A valid disability certificate and UDID card or enrollment number is required.
The annual family income should not exceed INR 8,00,000.
The graduate or postgraduate degree or diploma must be in Institutes of Excellence only.
The scholarship is awarded only once per student.
It is not for distance, part-time or weekend courses.
A maximum of two children with disabilities from the same parents can avail benefits.
Applicants cannot receive other scholarships simultaneously.

## Benefits
Tuition fees up to INR 1.90 lakh per annum.
Miscellaneous allowances up to INR 10,000 per annum.
Computer or laptop grant of INR 45,000 as a one-time grant in the first year.
Aids and assistive devices grant of INR 30,000 as a one-time grant in the first year.
Maintenance of INR 3,000 per month for hostellers or INR 1,500 per month for day scholars.
Special allowances of INR 2,000 per month.
Book allowance of INR 5,000 per annum.

## Documents Required
Applicant photo, previous academic marksheet, current year fee receipt, age verification certificate, family income certificate, bank account details and disability certificate.

## How to Apply
Visit the National Scholarship Portal (NSP), register as a new user and receive an application ID and password, log in with the credentials, open the application form in the Apply for Scholarship section, enter all the required details, upload the supporting documents and submit the application.

## Deadline
The scheme opens on June 1, 2026. The student application last date is October 31, 2026.
