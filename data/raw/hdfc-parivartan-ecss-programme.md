# HDFC Bank Parivartan ECSS Programme

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/parivartan-ecss-programme
Provider: HDFC Bank
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
The Parivartan ECSS Programme provides financial assistance to excellent students from impoverished backgrounds who are aspiring for a greater future.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be students in Classes 1 to 12, diploma, ITI, polytechnic, undergraduate or postgraduate courses in recognized institutions.
A minimum of 55% marks in the previous qualifying examination is required.
The annual family income should be less than or equal to INR 2.5 lakh.
Preference is given to applicants facing personal or family crises within the past three years that affected their education.
Indian nationals only.

## Benefits
Classes 1 to 6: INR 15,000.
Classes 7 to 12, Diploma, ITI and Polytechnic: INR 18,000.
Undergraduate General: INR 30,000.
Undergraduate Professional: INR 50,000.
Postgraduate General: INR 35,000.
Postgraduate Professional: INR 75,000.

## Documents Required
Passport size photograph, previous year's marksheets, identity proof, current year admission proof, bank passbook or cancelled cheque, income proof and proof of family or personal crisis if applicable.

## How to Apply
Apply online through the portal. Register or log in, complete the eligibility checklist, fill in the application form, upload the documents, review and submit.

## Deadline
31 December 2025.
