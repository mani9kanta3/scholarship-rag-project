# AICTE Saksham Scholarship Scheme

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/saksham-scholarship-scheme
Provider: All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A government initiative designed to support specially-abled students pursuing technical diploma or degree courses at AICTE-approved institutions by providing financial assistance for education related expenses.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be enrolled in the first year of a diploma or degree program, or the second year via lateral entry, at an AICTE-approved institution.
Applicants must be a specially-abled student with a minimum disability of 40%.
The annual family income from all sources should not exceed INR 8 lakh during the current financial year.
A valid income certificate is required from the State or UT Government.

## Benefits
INR 50,000 per annum is awarded for every year of study, up to a maximum of 4 years for students admitted in the first year and up to 3 years for students admitted directly into the second year through lateral entry.
It covers tuition fees, computers, stationery, books, equipment and software.
It is transferred via Direct Bank Transfer annually.

## Documents Required
Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, family income certificate, admission letter, tuition fee receipt, bank passbook copy, photograph, signature, Aadhaar card, institutional certificate, disability certificate, caste certificate if applicable and parent's declaration.

## How to Apply
Apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in. Register, log in, select AICTE Saksham Scholarship, enter the details, upload the documents and submit the application.

## Deadline
October 31, 2026.
