# National Fellowship and Scholarship for Higher Education of ST Students (NFST)

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/national-fellowship-and-scholarship-for-higher-education-of-st-students-nfst
Provider: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
This scheme promotes higher education among Scheduled Tribe students through two tracks. The National Fellowship Scheme supports M.Phil and Ph.D. study, and the National Scholarship Scheme supports graduate and postgraduate studies in professional fields like medicine, engineering, management and law.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be Indian nationals belonging to the Scheduled Tribe category.
For the fellowship, the applicant must be a maximum of 36 years old, admitted to a full-time M.Phil or Ph.D. course, and have a minimum of 55% marks in the postgraduate degree.
For the scholarship, the applicant must be pursuing studies in a premier institute and have an annual family income not exceeding INR 6,00,000.
Applicants cannot receive other government funding for the same level of study.

## Benefits
Fellowship recipients receive a stipend of up to INR 35,000 monthly.
An annual contingency grant of up to INR 25,000.
House rent allowance as per rules.
Scholarship awardees receive full tuition and admission fees, capped at INR 2,50,000 annually for private institutions.
A books allowance of INR 5,000 annually.
A monthly stipend of INR 3,000.
One-time computer assistance of INR 45,000.

## Documents Required
Fellowship applicants need a passport size photograph, ST certificate, Class 10 certificate, admission certificates and postgraduate marksheet.
Scholarship applicants need a fee receipt, qualifying exam marksheet, bonafide certificate, bank passbook copy, ST certificate and income certificate.

## How to Apply
Register on the National Tribal Fellowship Portal for the fellowship, or on the National Scholarship Portal for the scholarship. Upload the required documents and submit the application online through the respective portal.

## Deadline
October 31, 2026.
