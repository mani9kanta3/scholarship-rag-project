# ANRF-National Post Doctoral Fellowship (N-PDF)

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/scholarship/anrf-national-post-doctoral-fellowship-npdf
Provider: Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A prestigious initiative aimed at identifying and supporting motivated young researchers in the frontier areas of science, engineering, and medicine, with mentorship at recognised institutions.

## Eligibility
Applicants must hold Indian citizenship.
Age must not exceed 35 years at the time of application, with a 5 year relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, physically challenged and female candidates.
Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in Science or Engineering, or an M.D. or M.S. from a recognised university.
Applicants who have submitted their thesis and are awaiting the award of the degree are also eligible.
The fellowship can only be availed once per career.
Applicants cannot apply to multiple schemes in the same calendar year.

## Benefits
INR 80,000 per month plus applicable HRA, or INR 50,000 for pending degree holders.
A research grant of INR 2,00,000 per year.
An overhead of INR 1,00,000 per year.
The fellowship is tenable for 2 years only in India.

## Documents Required
Applicant bio-data and photograph.
Ph.D., M.D. or M.S. degree certificate.
Research proposal details.
NOC if employed.

## How to Apply
Click on the Apply Now button, register or log in, go to Online Application Procedure, fill in the details, upload the documents and click Submit.

## Deadline
20th February 2026. The status shown on the page is Closed.
