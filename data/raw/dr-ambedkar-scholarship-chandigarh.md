# Dr Ambedkar Scholarship, Chandigarh

Source URL: https://www.buddy4study.com/article/dr-ambedkar-scholarship-chandigarh
Provider: Chandigarh Administration under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India
Fetched on: 2026-08-25

## Description
A centrally sponsored post-matric scholarship initiative supporting economically disadvantaged Backward Class students pursuing higher education from Class 11 to PhD levels, named after the architect of the Indian Constitution.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be Indian citizens and permanent Chandigarh residents.
Applicants must belong to the EBC categories per the Government of India's list.
Applicants must be enrolled in post-matriculation courses at recognised institutions.
The annual household income must not exceed INR 2,50,000.
No specific minimum marks are required, but continuation requires 75% attendance.
Applicants cannot avail other central pre-matric scholarships.
There are special provisions for students with disabilities.

## Benefits
Monthly maintenance allowances by group. Hostellers receive INR 260 to INR 750 and day scholars receive INR 160 to INR 350.
Reimbursement for mandatory institutional fees.
Additional reader charges for blind scholars of INR 90 to INR 175 per month.
Study tour expenses up to INR 900 annually.
Thesis typing and printing reimbursement up to INR 1,000.

## Documents Required
Class 10 and 12 marksheets, family income certificate issued after April 1, disability certificate if applicable, fee receipts, bank passbook copies, Aadhaar card, institution certificate and parent's declaration.

## How to Apply
Register on the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in via the OTR process using a mobile number and Aadhaar authentication, then apply for the scholarship with the required documents.
